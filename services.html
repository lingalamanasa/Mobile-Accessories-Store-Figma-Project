Hardware Care & Longevity Protocols

Simple routines to keep your STACKLY accessories performing like day one for years.

Aramid Case Care

Wipe with a damp microfiber cloth and mild soap. Ballistic fibers repel oils naturally, so harsh solvents are never needed.

GaN Pin Maintenance

Keep USB-C ports clean with low-pressure air. Gold-plated contacts ensure oxidation resistance across 10,000 insertions.

MagBattery Storage

Store lithium-ion magnetic banks at 50% charge in a cool, dry area if not in daily use to maximize cell longevity.
