Technical Q&A: Your Hardware Questions Answered

Ravi Kumar, Hardware Lead
On Fast-Charging Longevity

"Does charging at 65W or 100W harm smartphone batteries?"

No, modern smartphones negotiate charging wattage via bidirectional USB-PD handshake protocols. The phone requests only what its internal battery controller can safely absorb. Wattage steps down automatically once the battery reaches 80% to avoid thermal stress.

Ananya Rao, Head of Design
On MagSafe Alignment

"Why do standard magnetic cases slip off car mounts?"

Most generic cases use low-grade N35 magnets positioned flat. STACKLY uses 38 custom N52 neodymium magnets arrayed in a Halbach pattern. This focuses magnetic flux toward the mount rather than letting it bleed outward, delivering 2.4x greater shear resistance.
