Our Genesis

Honest hardware for daily drivers.

In 2019, our founder Ravi V. was working on thermal mitigation systems for aerospace sensors in Hyderabad. During a field test, his phone fell from hip height onto asphalt — inside an expensive branded case that claimed 'military drop-testing.' The phone shattered completely.

Taking the broken case into the testing lab revealed what the industry hid: 0.8mm brittle polystyrene backed with cheap polyurethane foam that degraded under ambient heat. The marketing was brilliant; the engineering was fraudulent.

STACKLY began on that bench with a single rule: Never use decorative materials where structural physics are required.

Design Philosophy

Three Uncompromising Engineering Principles

Every STACKLY product must satisfy all three criteria
Principle 01

Durability Over Aesthetics

Form must follow survival. We never shave structural wall thickness to look 0.2mm slimmer in a catalog. Every chamfer serves to redirect kinetic impact energy away from fragile display corners.

Metric: Kinetic Dissipation Rate > 85%
Principle 02

Material Honesty

If it looks like metal, it is CNC billet aluminum. If it looks like carbon, it is 1500D ballistic Aramid. We never apply cosmetic vinyl stickers or metallic spray paint over brittle plastic skeletons.

Metric: 100% Solid Billet & Genuine Weave
Principle 03

Zero Waste Responsibility

Hardware should outlast multiple phone upgrades. Every old STACKLY case sent back to our Hyderabad recycling hub is melted down to produce polymer spacers for future production runs.

Metric: Circular Trade-In Credit Active
